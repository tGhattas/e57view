      Moves the active layer onto the reference and never touches the reference. Match centres and Match scales are the coarse step; ICP is point-to-plane and lands on the reference's own surfaces. Distance to reference writes a scalar field on the active layer, so the colour ramp and the histogram show where the two disagree.

      —

      How this works

      Click Create region then click a point on the cloud: a small shape appears there, ready to resize. Grow, the handles, the size sliders, or Alt + scroll while it is active all enlarge it; Fit to contents grows it until it stops finding new points and then tightens onto them.

      Drag the arrows to move, the rings to rotate, the handles to resize. Double-click a point to move the orbit centre, then centre the region there. Keep inside dims everything outside the region; Remove inside dims what is inside it — either way the preview is what Apply will leave behind. Escape cancels the region.

      Sections

        —

        How this works

        Bounding shapes, combined as a union: keep only what lies inside any of them, or mark one Remove to drop what is inside it. A section is a slab you move, tilt and thicken with the gizmo — floors, a wall, a corridor. A drawn region (S) is an outline extruded along the direction you drew it from, so you can orbit around it and see exactly what it holds.

        Clean

        Noise filter options

        —

        CloudCompare's SOR and noise filters, same settings, same points removed.

        How this works

        Remove outliers measures each point's mean distance to its neighbours and drops the ones beyond the mean plus Strictness standard deviations, judged over the whole cloud. A few very distant points can push that threshold out past the real outliers, so crop a stray reflection away first.

        Remove noise fits a plane to the points around each point and drops it if it sits further off that plane than its own neighbours' spread allows. The threshold is local, so a smooth wall is judged strictly and a hedge loosely. Real edges survive because they still fit a plane.

        Every removal is one undoable step.

          Opens a PLY, OBJ or STL as a layer of its own, to compare against or edit.

          How this works

          Build from points is surface reconstruction: it needs normals, and writes a triangle mesh as a separate object that you can show, hide, measure or export. Detail is the voxel size, so smaller is finer and slower, and below the scan's own point spacing it only adds noise. Fill gaps widens the band each point writes, closing small holes at the cost of rounding sharp edges.

          Area and volume are measured through the layer's transform. A volume only means anything when the mesh is closed, so the boundary edge count is reported next to it. Smooth is Taubin with Keep volume on and plain Laplacian with it off. Decimate is vertex clustering at the cell you set, which is fast but cannot hit an exact triangle count.

          Distance from this cloud measures every point of the active point layer to the nearest triangle of the mesh layer you pick, and writes it as a scalar field.
